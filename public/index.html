How to play

  1. Pick a pathogen and a patient-zero country. You start with a few DNA points.
  2. Evolve transmission vectors (air, water, insects…) to jump between countries, and symptoms to raise severity and lethality.
  3. Spread happens automatically — infections grow, then travel by land, air and sea. Click a country on the map to inspect it.
  4. Cure research starts once humanity notices you. High severity makes you noticeable and deadly, but speeds up the cure — balance is everything.
  5. Planes and ships carry your pathogen between countries — watch them move. Closed borders only slow traffic, never stop it.
  6. People recover. Health systems clear cases, and recovery speeds up as cure research advances. Kill quickly or lose your hosts.
  7. Human resistance grows wherever the outbreak is noticed — strongest in rich, healthy countries. It blocks new infections and blunts lethality. Fight it with Abilities such as Immunosuppression, Antigenic Shift and Drug Tolerance.
  8. Win by killing everyone before the cure reaches 100%.
  • Drag to pan, scroll or double-click to zoom.
  • Space = pause/resume. Keys 1–4 set game speed.
  • Small, poor, tropical countries are easiest to hide in early game.
  • Panicked countries close their borders — spread early, kill late.
  • Ten pathogens play very differently: a prion is nearly invisible for decades, a nanobot swarm ignores immunity, a prion or bio-weapon trades stealth for raw lethality.
  • The map shows resistance: countries drift steel blue as their populations adapt.
